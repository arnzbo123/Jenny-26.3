Goodcraft Fabric 26.3 migration development build

This is NOT the completed mod and does not preserve full gameplay yet.

Implemented: Fabric initialization, legacy configuration storage, persistent
fire, sound registry, and converted resources. GeckoLib 5.5.7 can parse and
bake all 26 supplied models and 406 animation clips. Playback controllers and
renderers are not implemented, so this does not mean animations play in game.

Still missing: NPC/player entities, bosses, spawns, structures, items and their
interactions, inventories/screens, network handlers, commands, and save migration.

Do not use this development artifact as a replacement in an existing modded
world. See MIGRATION.md and migration/FEATURES.md in the development workspace.
